Welcome to NHL Payroll Management System

System Basics:

The NHL Payroll Management System is designed to automate the payroll process for organizations. In many companies, payroll is done manually which leads to errors, delays, and loss of data.

This system will manage Employees, Departments,salary,Position,payslips and Payrolls. It allows HR to add new employees, assign them to departments,Their Position, set their basic salary, calculate allowances and deductions, and generate monthly payslips. Employees can log in to view their dashboard, check their salary history and download payslips.
